## What it is, in plain language
Most AI tools answer questions. An **agent** goes a step further: you give it a job, and it does the steps itself, then checks in when it needs a decision from you. Think of it as the difference between asking a friend for directions and asking a friend to drive.

**Grok Bot** is xAI's agent app. Each "Bot" is like a teammate with its own computer in the cloud. You sign it into the apps and websites you already use, and it works inside them the way you would: reading, sorting, drafting, filling in, and reporting back. Because that computer lives in the cloud, Bots keep working around the clock, in parallel, even when your own computer is off.

A few things make it practical for a business owner:
- **Bots take one job each.** You can have an inbox Bot, a research Bot and a reporting Bot, and add more as the work grows.
- **You stay in control.** When an action needs your OK, such as sending an email, the Bot shows an approval card and waits.
- **You can show it how.** xAI says you can show a Bot a workflow once and it remembers the steps.
- **It works where your team works.** Bots can work in Slack like a teammate, and you can invite one to a Google Meet or Zoom call to listen, help in the chat and follow up afterward.
- **It never sees your passwords.** When a task needs a login, the Bot uses a saved login or a password manager such as 1Password, or asks you through a secure form. For steps like a passkey, it hands the computer to you.

## How to get started
1. **Pick a plan that includes Grok Bot.** Under The X Subscription, Grok Bot is included on **Premium, Super and Ultra**. The Free and Plus plans don't include it (see pricing below).
2. **Download the app** from [x.ai/bot](https://x.ai/bot). It runs on macOS, Windows and Linux. There are also iPhone (iOS 18 or later) and Android (Android 9 or later) apps that reach the same Bots, so you can approve actions from your phone.
3. **Sign in.** Choose Sign in and finish in your browser. Grok Bot uses your Cursor account, so if your company already uses single sign-on, that applies.
4. **Meet your first Bot.** After a short intro, the app creates a Bot named Grok Bot and opens a chat. You can also start from a ready-made Bot. xAI's examples include one that watches the web for mentions of your brand and one that turns meeting notes into a recap deck.
5. **Hand it a first task**, then turn it into a routine (next section).

## Recommended setup: an inbox Bot that saves you 30+ minutes a day
Email is where most owners lose their mornings. This setup gives you a sorted inbox and drafted replies before you sit down, and nothing goes out without you.

**Step 1: Create a Bot with one job.** Press the + button (or Cmd/Ctrl+N), name it "Inbox Manager" and choose Create. xAI's own guidance is to give each Bot one job in its first message. A focused Bot learns your preferences faster than a catch-all helper.

**Step 2: Connect your email.** Ask the Bot to connect your email. For supported services you can connect the app directly. Otherwise the Bot signs in on its own computer through a secure form, so it never sees the password.

**Step 3: Give it the job, the rules and the limits.** Copy and adapt this:

> You are my inbox manager. Every weekday at 7:00 AM, sort my unread email into three groups: Urgent (customers, payments, anything with a deadline this week), Needs a reply, and FYI. For Urgent and Needs a reply, draft a short reply in my voice: friendly, direct, no more than five sentences. Never send anything. Never delete anything. Give me a summary I can read in two minutes with the drafts ready to approve. Remember that [Client A] and [Supplier B] are always Urgent.

**Step 4: Review the first run and correct it.** Tell it what it got wrong ("Newsletters are always FYI", "Sign my replies 'Thanks, [your name]'"). Ask it to remember your preferences.

**Step 5: Make it a routine.** When the result looks right, ask it to run on a schedule. In Grok Bot, a task on a schedule becomes a routine you can pause or resume from your phone.

**Step 6: Approve from anywhere.** When a draft is ready, approve or edit it from the desktop or mobile app.

Starter variations xAI suggests that also work well as a first task:
- **Morning briefing:** "Sum up what changed since yesterday in my inbox, calendar, Slack, and docs, and flag what needs me. Don't send anything."
- **Competitor check:** "Check these three competitors for anything they launched or repriced this week. Send me a short list with links."

## Ideas to spark your own
- **Trades (plumbing, HVAC, electrical):** a Bot that drafts follow-ups to every quote you sent but haven't heard back on in five days, ready for you to approve.
- **Retail and e-commerce:** a weekly competitor watch on prices, new products and promotions, with links.
- **Restaurants:** start from a mention-watching Bot to get a weekday digest when your restaurant is talked about online, so you can respond to reviews faster.
- **Agencies:** show a Bot your weekly client report once (pull the numbers, paste them into your template), then let it repeat that every Monday.
- **Professional services:** invite a Bot to client calls. It takes notes, then drafts the recap and next-step email afterward.
- **Bookkeeping and admin:** an invoice Bot that checks new invoices against purchase orders and flags the ones that don't match. That workflow appears in xAI's own examples.
- **Customer support:** a Bot that works your helpdesk queue and drafts replies to common tickets for your review.
- **Hiring:** a Bot that screens applicants against your must-haves and gives you a shortlist with reasons.
- **Sales:** an overnight Bot that researches leads and drafts outreach for you to approve in the morning.

## Your setup to grow into
- **Week 1, Premium ($30/mo):** One Inbox Manager Bot on a morning routine. Measure the time you get back.
- **Weeks 2–4, still Premium:** Add a second and third Bot, each with one job, such as quote follow-ups and a weekly report. Teach each one a workflow by showing it once. Watch your weekly usage in settings.
- **Month 2, Super ($100/mo) when you hit the weekly limit:** Super gives far more weekly usage and priority access at busy times. Upgrade when your Bots run daily and you keep reaching the cap, not before.
- **Month 3+, Teams seats:** Give staff their own seats, put Bots in your Slack channels and let Bots hand work to each other, for example an Invoice Bot passing to an AP Bot. You can mix seat tiers.
- **Later, Ultra or Enterprise:** For many Bots working in parallel all day, or when you need invoicing and advanced security.

The best signal that it's worth paying more is simple: your Bots keep running out of weekly allowance doing work you would otherwise do yourself.
