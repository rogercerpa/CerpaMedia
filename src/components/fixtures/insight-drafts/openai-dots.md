## What it is, in plain language
A regular ChatGPT conversation waits for you to type. An **agent** is different: you give it a goal and it figures out the steps, does the work, and comes back with results or questions.

OpenAI launched **dots** on **September 29, 2026**. A dot is your personal, always-on agent inside ChatGPT. In plain terms:
- **It has its own computer in the cloud,** with a web browser, so it can do things online for you while your laptop is closed.
- **It connects to your apps.** OpenAI says dots can reach more than 4,000 apps through its plugins. You choose which ones.
- **It remembers.** It starts with what ChatGPT already knows about you (if memory is on) and learns how you like things done.
- **It keeps going.** It can research in the background, run scheduled checks, and message you with updates and decisions that need your judgment.
- **You can reach it where you are:** in ChatGPT on web, desktop and mobile, in Slack or Microsoft Teams, or on a voice call from the app. Texting is in a limited beta for some Pro users in the US, Canada and Mexico.

You get **one personal dot** for now. OpenAI says you'll later be able to add more and scale how much work each one takes on. Dots are powered by OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra, and since Nov 3 they can also draw on GPT-6.1 Terra.

## Who can get one (as of Nov 17, 2026)
- **ChatGPT Pro:** for users aged 18 and older, outside the European Economic Area, Switzerland and the UK. It's rolling out gradually, so access can take several days.
- **ChatGPT Business, Premium seats:** in supported regions, subject to workspace eligibility. OpenAI's help pages mention Business Premium users. Standard seats are not listed.
- **ChatGPT Enterprise, Edu and Healthcare:** admins could enable dots during a preview from Nov 4 to Nov 18, 2026. From **Nov 18**, dots are on by default unless the admin opts out.
- **Free, Go and Plus:** dots are not included.

## How to get started
1. **Get on an eligible plan** (see pricing below).
2. **Create your dot** in the ChatGPT desktop app (Mac or Windows), on desktop web, or in the latest ChatGPT mobile app on iOS or Android. Mobile web isn't supported. Follow the onboarding prompts, give it a name and pick a character or pet as its avatar.
3. **Connect your apps.** Open your dot's profile, go to **Customize > Plugins**, pick an app and review its permissions before connecting. You can connect Slack from ChatGPT on desktop and connect your personal email account. Start with the one or two apps your first task needs.
4. **Set your rules.** In **Customize > Custom rules**, decide how your dot handles actions like sharing, purchasing or accessing things. For each rule you choose one of four options: take action without asking, take action if pre-approved, ask before taking action, or hand off to you. For anything customer-facing, start with "ask before taking action".
5. **Give it a task** by describing the goal and sharing the details it needs. Track progress in its profile under In progress, Scheduled and Completed. You can pause your dot any time from the ••• menu.

Good to know: **conversations with your dot don't count toward your ChatGPT usage limits.** Tasks your dot starts in ChatGPT Work or Codex do count, as usual.

## Recommended setup: a follow-up dot that makes sure no lead goes cold
Most businesses lose money in the gap between "send me a quote" and "we never heard back." This setup closes that gap.

**Step 1: Connect your email and calendar** under Customize > Plugins. Grant read access first. Leave sending off until you trust the drafts.

**Step 2: Add a custom rule:** "Sending email: Ask before taking action."

**Step 3: Give your dot the responsibility.** Copy and adapt this:

> Your job is to make sure no customer or lead goes cold. Every weekday at 8:00 AM, check my email for anyone I sent a quote, proposal or estimate to who hasn't replied in 4 or more business days. For each one, draft a short, friendly follow-up in my voice that references what we discussed and asks one easy question. Also flag any customer email I haven't answered in 24 hours. Put everything in one message for me with the drafts ready. Do not send anything without my approval. Keep a running list of who you've followed up with and when.

**Step 4: Turn it into a scheduled task.** Your dot will set up the recurring check. You'll find it under Scheduled, where you can change the time or pause it.

**Step 5: Review and give feedback in the conversation.** For example: "Shorter," "Don't follow up with vendors," "Mention our 2-week lead time." Your dot learns from these corrections.

**Step 6: Expand once it's reliable.** For example, ask it to also draft a thank-you note after every completed job.

## Pricing tiers (as of Nov 17, 2026)
Your first dot is included in eligible plans at no extra cost, along with an allowance for deeper work. OpenAI has not published a separate per-dot price for Pro or Business users.

| Plan | Price | Dot included? | What you get | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free, Go, Plus | Varies | No | Regular ChatGPT; no dot | Not an option for dots today |
| **Pro 100** | **$100/mo (monthly only)** | **Yes** | One dot, Pro reasoning, Codex and ChatGPT Work, deep research | **A solo owner starting with dots** |
| Pro 200 | $200/mo | Yes | More Work and Codex usage than Pro 100 | Owners whose dot does heavy research or document work daily |
| Pro 500 | $500/mo | Yes | Highest usage, plus Ultrafast models | Power users who need speed and volume |
| Business, Standard seat | $25/user/mo, or $20/user/mo billed annually | Not listed | Shared team workspace, ChatGPT Work, Codex | Staff who don't need a dot |
| **Business, Premium seat** | **$125/user/mo, or $100/user/mo billed annually** | **Yes** | Higher usage limits, no five-hour usage limit, admin controls, no training on your data | **Teams.** Give Premium seats to the people who need a dot |
| Enterprise | Contact sales | Yes | Admin controls, flat-rate dot billing | Larger organizations |

Notes:
- A Business workspace needs **at least 2 seats** (Standard, Premium or one of each). The smallest Business setup with a dot is one Premium seat plus one Standard seat: $150/mo billed monthly or $120/mo billed annually.
- Pro and Business can buy credits for eligible usage beyond the included allowance.
- On Enterprise, dots are free through **Jan 13, 2027**. Billing starts **Jan 14, 2027**, at flat rates by type of work.

## Ideas to spark your own
- **Contractors and trades:** each evening, your dot reviews tomorrow's jobs on your calendar, checks the weather for outdoor work and drafts "running late / on our way" messages ready to approve.
- **Retail:** a weekly check of your best sellers' stock levels and supplier emails, with a reorder list waiting Monday morning.
- **Restaurants:** your dot reads new reviews, drafts replies in your voice and writes up the complaint themes for your weekly staff meeting.
- **Content and creators:** hand over an interview or podcast transcript. Your dot finds the best moments and drafts show notes and social posts. OpenAI uses this example itself.
- **Agencies:** before each client call, your dot builds a one-page brief from recent emails and project files.
- **Professional services (accountants, lawyers, consultants):** a document-chase dot that tracks which clients still owe paperwork and drafts polite reminders.
- **Software and online businesses:** a dot that watches customer feedback and turns it into prioritized fixes. OpenAI describes a developer doing exactly this.
- **Real estate:** your dot researches comparable listings and prepares a summary before every showing.
- **Owners with presentations to give:** your dot pulls data and customer feedback together and keeps the deck updated as new information comes in.

## Your setup to grow into
- **Start on Pro 100 ($100/mo)** with one dot and one responsibility, such as follow-ups. Connect only the apps that task needs.
- **Add responsibilities one at a time:** a morning calendar brief, review replies, a weekly numbers recap. Use custom rules to give it more independence as it earns your trust.
- **Move up to Pro 200 or Pro 500** if your dot keeps reaching its allowance for deeper work, such as long research, documents or code.
- **Bring your team in with ChatGPT Business.** Premium seats for the people who need their own dot, Standard seats for everyone else, and your dot in Slack or Teams where the work happens.
- **Watch for what's coming:** OpenAI says it plans to let you add more dots and has previewed "specialist dots" that take on defined roles inside a company. Those aren't generally available yet.
