## What it is, in plain language
Most AI tools wait for you to open them and ask. An **agent** does the work itself: you give it a job, it works out the steps, and it comes back when it's done or needs your decision.

Anthropic announced **Claude Duet** on **October 21, 2026**. It takes the agent idea one step further: Duet is **proactive**, so it comes to you first. Anthropic says it built Duet for professionals and business owners with more on their plates than they can track. In plain terms:
- **It briefs you every morning.** You get a short summary of the most important items across your connected apps.
- **It keeps watch.** During the day it checks your connected accounts every few hours and messages you only when something needs you. You can tell it what you want to hear about and how often.
- **It takes on real tasks,** from scheduling meetings to building dashboards, sales or financial forecasts, and even simple web apps. It runs on its own cloud computer with a browser, so the work continues when your laptop and phone are off.
- **It uses the tools your business runs on.** Anthropic says Duet can use more than 6,000 connectors and plugins in the Claude Marketplace, including Gmail, Slack, QuickBooks, Stripe, Gusto, HubSpot, Docusign, Canva, LinkedIn and Shopify.
- **Big decisions stay with you.** Duet reads, drafts and prepares on its own. Before it sends a message, makes a payment, signs a document or deletes anything, it shows you exactly what it will do and waits for your OK. Some things are off-limits even with your approval, such as trading securities or moving money between accounts.
- **It never directly sees your passwords or sign-in tokens.** It can buy things for your business through Link, Stripe's wallet, which pays with a virtual card after you approve the spend.

It's powered by Claude Opus 5.5. You talk to it by text or voice in one ongoing thread, in a standalone iOS or Android app, or on the web at claude.ai.

**Be realistic:** this is a research preview. Anthropic itself says Duet may misread a thread, misjudge what's urgent, or miss something that arrives between check-ins. It also says personal errands are weaker than work tasks, and that you can't yet watch its browser.

## How to get started
1. **Have (or get) an eligible plan:** Claude **Pro, Max or Ultra**, in the **United States**. Team and Enterprise plans aren't included yet.
2. **Join the waitlist** from the [Claude Duet page](https://claude.com/product/claude-duet). Invitations go out in waves, and you'll get an email when it's your turn.
3. **Get ready while you wait.** This is the step most people skip. Duet carries over your **memory, skills and connected apps** from your regular Claude account. In the Claude app, connect the tools your first task needs (for example your email, calendar, and QuickBooks or Stripe), and spend a week using Claude so it learns your business. On day one, Duet will already know your context.
4. **When invited,** install the Claude Duet app on your phone or open it at claude.ai, then send your first message.
5. **Tell it what matters.** Say which topics you want flagged and how often. Remember that more frequent checking uses your plan's limits faster.

## Recommended setup: get paid faster with an invoice-chasing routine
Late payments drain owners' time and cash. Anthropic lists "check QuickBooks or Stripe for unpaid invoices and draft reminders" as a core Duet task, so it's a strong first job.

**Step 1: Connect your books and your email.** Connect QuickBooks or Stripe and your Gmail in Claude (or ask Duet to connect them).

**Step 2: Hand it the job.** Copy and adapt this:

> Every Monday and Thursday morning, check QuickBooks for invoices that are past due. For each one, draft a reminder email from me: polite for 1–14 days late, firmer for 15–30 days, and for anything over 30 days, flag it in my morning brief instead of drafting. Include the invoice number, amount, due date and payment link. Don't send anything until I approve. Keep a running list of who has been reminded and when, and tell me the total outstanding each week.

**Step 3: Approve from your phone.** Duet shows each email exactly as it will be sent. Approve, edit or say "not now."

**Step 4: Tune the morning brief.** After a few days, tell it what to add or drop: "Always include today's deposits," "Skip newsletters," "Flag any customer who emails twice in one day."

**Step 5: Add a second job.** A natural next step is drafting follow-ups to recent leads from your CRM history, such as HubSpot. Or ask for a one-page dashboard you can check every morning (Anthropic's own demo shows exactly this).

## Pricing tiers (as of Nov 17, 2026)
During the research preview, **Duet is included in Pro, Max and Ultra at no extra charge.** It shares your plan's usage limits with the rest of Claude. Background checks count toward those limits, and big jobs like a financial model or a deck use more. Anthropic has not announced separate Duet pricing for after the preview. Prices exclude tax.

| Plan | Price | Duet? | What you get | Who it's for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | No | Chat on web, desktop and mobile; connectors; memory | Getting familiar with Claude before you upgrade |
| **Pro** | **$20/mo, or $200/yr billed up front (about $17/mo)** | **Yes (waitlist, US)** | More usage, scheduled tasks, Claude Code, projects, Claude in Chrome and Microsoft 365 | **Owners starting with one or two Duet routines** |
| Max | $100/mo (monthly billing) | Yes (waitlist, US) | 5x Pro usage, priority access at busy times, $100/mo in API credits | Owners who lean on Duet all day |
| Ultra | $200/mo (monthly billing) | Yes (waitlist, US) | 10x Pro weekly usage, no 5-hour limit for verified accounts, $200/mo in API credits | Heavy users running long, complex jobs |
| Team / Enterprise | See claude.com/pricing | Not yet | Shared workspaces and admin controls | Not an option for Duet today |

On paid plans, you can also turn on usage credits to keep working at standard API rates once you hit a limit.

## Ideas to spark your own
- **Real estate:** a morning brief that catches calendar mistakes, such as a showing booked on the wrong day. One early tester, a broker, says Duet caught exactly that without being asked.
- **Restaurants with multiple locations:** work through supplier invoices store by store and summarize customer complaints. An early tester who owns a restaurant group with about 110 employees uses Duet for accounting, support and marketing.
- **Consultants and strategists:** Duet notices an upcoming client call, prepares a brief, and flags if it clashes with travel.
- **Trades and field services:** Duet tracks open quotes, drafts follow-ups, and prepares agreements to send for signature through Docusign once you approve.
- **E-commerce:** Duet helps set up and manage a Shopify store, or turns a product launch into a social campaign in Canva.
- **Owners with staff:** Duet prepares payroll in Gusto with hours, bonuses and reimbursements filled in, for you to review before it runs.
- **Hiring:** Duet drafts a job posting, then publishes it to LinkedIn after you edit and approve it.
- **Opening a new location:** Duet builds the opening checklist from your past openings and tracks permits, installs and deadlines. Anthropic's demo walks through exactly this.
- **Any owner:** "Build me one page I can check every morning: is the business healthy, and where should I look?"

## Your setup to grow into
- **Now, Free or Pro ($20/mo):** Set up connectors and memory in Claude so you're ready. Join the Duet waitlist with Pro.
- **Week 1 with Duet, Pro:** One routine (invoices or follow-ups) plus the morning brief. Note how much time and how many reminders it saves.
- **Month 1, still Pro:** Add a weekly dashboard and a second routine. Tell Duet to check in less often if you're hitting limits.
- **Move to Max ($100/mo) when Duet becomes your daily operator:** when it runs several routines and larger projects (forecasts, decks, store setup) and Pro limits slow you down.
- **Ultra ($200/mo):** For long, complex work that can't stop at a 5-hour limit.
- **Teams later:** Anthropic hasn't announced Team or Enterprise access to Duet yet. Watch for it if you want staff on it too.
